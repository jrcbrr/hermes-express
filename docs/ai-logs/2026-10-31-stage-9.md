❯ question for #1 what will be the process for mobile users                     

● I'm taking #1 to mean the Perfect Pour game, since the demo passwords work the
  same on any device. On a phone, it would go like this:

  1. Open it: on the Base step of /build, the customer taps "Try the Perfect
     Pour". It's optional, so they can ignore it and tap Next as usual.
  2. See the game: a cup with a gold line appears, with a large "Hold to pour"
     button in easy thumb reach at 375px and clear of the bottom tab bar. A
     Close button is always visible.
  3. Pour: they press and hold the button with a thumb, and the liquid rises at
     a steady speed with no timer.
  4. Release: letting go stops the pour.
     - On the gold line: a small margin counts as a win, because exact precision
       is hard on a touchscreen. The Perfect Pour badge unlocks with the usual
       toast.
     - Short or past the line: a warm message explains what happened and offers
       "Try again", with unlimited retries. If they keep holding, the pour stops
       at the brim and waits for them to let go.
  5. Return: Close takes them back to the Base step with their drink unchanged.

  Phone problems I'll handle
  - Long press: holding a finger down can normally select text or open a menu,
    so I'll turn that off on the button.
  - Scrolling: a slight finger movement during the hold won't scroll the page or
    end the pour.
  - Interruptions: if a call or app switch interrupts the hold, the attempt
    resets without counting as a miss, and they can simply pour again.

  Guests: a guest who wins sees a login link, but the win isn't saved. They'd
  need to win again after logging in to get the badge. I'll also check whether
  their drink in progress survives the login trip, and fix it if not.

  The E2E test will run this with touch input on the 375px mobile setup, in
  addition to the keyboard test on desktop.

  Reply "go" to start with the plan and my three recommendations as written, or
  tell me what to change.
